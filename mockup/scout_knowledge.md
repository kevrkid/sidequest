# What Side Quest can do (Scout's app knowledge)

Keep this file up to date when features change. Only describe things that exist today.

## Accounts and guests
- Anyone can look around without an account: browse everything and RSVP. Anything that reaches
  other people (posting, waving, messaging, joining a group, going live, reporting, chatting with
  Scout beyond basics) asks them to make a free account, then takes them back to what they were doing.
  Whatever they did as a guest carries into the account. Sign-up needs 18+ and accepting the Terms.
  During the beta, sign-up may need an invite code.
- The screen (V3): the map fills the whole screen, always. Over it, at the bottom, a drawer you drag up and down
  (three stops: just the search box, half way, all the way up), and under that a bar with five buttons:
  **Explore** (the map and what's around), **For you** (the feed), **＋** (create), **Chats**, and **You** (your picture).
- Top of the screen, floating on the map: your picture at the top left (opens You; a green ring = you show as
  online), the Side Quest name in the middle (takes you back to the map), and the 🔔 bell at the top right. Down the
  right side: map layers, ghost / friends location sharing, and "back to where I am".
- Guests: tapping You (or your picture) offers Create account / Sign in.

## Explore (the drawer over the map)
- The search box at the top: "Search Austin, or ask Scout". It finds places, parks, lakes, trails, sights, events,
  people and groups as you type, nearest first; "Find it on menus" searches every menu for a dish; a question
  ("where's good tacos open late?") goes to you, Scout. The 🎤 in the box opens your voice mode.
- Under it, chips: 🍔 Food, 🎫 Tonight, 🍸 Nightlife, 🌳 Outdoors, 🏛️ Sights, 👥 Friends, 🔥 Hot spots. A chip shows that
  one thing on the map AND lists it in the drawer (nearest first), with smaller chips to narrow it (Food: Happy hour,
  Coffee, Tacos, BBQ, Pizza, Burgers, Asian, Trucks, Sweets, Late night, Breakfast, plus an Open now switch; Tonight:
  This weekend / This week; Nightlife: Bars, Clubs, Late eats; Outdoors: Parks, Lakes & rivers, Trails; Sights:
  Landmarks, Museums, Markers, Art). Tap a row to fly to it on the map. ✕ or tapping the chip again goes back.
- One line under the chips says what's on ("37 things on tonight · 2 friends out"); tap it to pull the drawer up.
- Pulled up, the drawer shows: stories (rings), Up next (the next thing you're going to, with a countdown),
  Friends out now, Tonight near you, Busy right now (hot spots), Happy hour now, and quick buttons (My plans, your
  level and coins). Dragging the map moves the drawer out of the way.

## Create (the ＋ in the middle of the bottom bar)
- Story (a photo or video for 24 hours), Post (a public post with a photo or text that stays on your profile and
  in For you → Posts), Flare, Event, Group, Go live.

## Chats (bottom bar)
- Tabs: 💬 Chats (messages and group chats; friends only), 👥 Friends (requests, your friends, people you'd click with,
  find anyone by name or interest), 🏘️ Groups, 👋 Meet (the swipe deck). The red number on Chats counts unread
  messages plus friend requests.

## You (bottom bar)
- Your picture and name, the Online/Offline switch, your level, coins, day streak and friends, then My plans, Saved,
  Friends, Quests & rewards, Invite friends and Español. Below that, four tabs: Profile, Personality (quiz +
  astrology), About you, Privacy (location precision, delay, your data, blocked people, sign out, delete account).
- Online = friends and Meet see you as around and your location dot stays live; Offline = nobody sees you as active
  and the app stops using your location.

## Feed
- A ranked list of real Austin events (from ~45 local sources, refreshed every 6 hours), flares,
  group posts, people and groups, each with reasons ("You're into comedy · 2 friends going").
- When it's sorted "For you" (the default), the top shows "Tonight's picks for you": up to 3 flares
  picked for them (by Claude when signed in), each with a match %, reasons, I'm down / Show on map /
  Not tonight, and a ↻ to pick again. The For you button in the bottom bar opens this.
- ⚙️ Customize: content types, categories, sort, distance. 🗺 Map it shows the list on the map.

## Map
- Event pins, flares, story bubbles, live-stream thumbnails, hot spots, parks and lakes, farmers markets and your
  blue location dot. It starts calm (what's happening); the chips under the search box bring in food, nightlife,
  sights and more. Other people's positions are venue-level or blurred about a quarter mile on purpose.

## Meet (Chats → 👋 Meet)
- One swipe deck: people (right = wave; you both wave = friends), events (right = going),
  groups (right = join), flares (right = I'm down). Left = pass, and there's undo.
- ⚙️ Sort & filter: activity, when, distance, goals, interests, new to Austin, from my groups, online now.
- The 🔎 box under the tabs narrows whatever tab is open.

## Stories, plans, flares
- Stories: the row of rings at the top of Explore (and in the feed). Tap one for a full-screen feed like TikTok:
  swipe up for the next story, tap a video to pause, double-tap to like ❤️, and use the buttons on the right to
  reply (friends only), share, or see the spot on the map. 🔇 turns sound on.
- Plans: You → 📅 My plans (or the My plans button in Explore) shows saved itineraries and events you're going to.
  When someone asks you to plan their night, day or weekend, build a plan (see the plan rules you're given); they can
  save it, see it on the map, add it to their calendar or share it.
- Story: a photo, a video up to 60 seconds, or text; lasts 24 hours. "Where was this?"
  tags a place you've been or one nearby; you can also tag an event, groups and people.
- Flare: a "who's down?" post for right now (＋ → Flare). Event: something for a later date and time. Group: start a
  community. Go live.

## Live
- Stream from the phone camera (front or back, flip mid-stream), USB webcam mode, Bluetooth pairing
  for GoPro / Insta360 / DJI, and Wi-Fi/RTMP instructions. Viewers send requests (visit a place or
  do a task, with play-money tips). Claude screens every request for safety first.
- Action cameras and OBS: Go live → 📶 Wi-Fi (RTMP) → pick the camera brand → "Get my camera address",
  then paste the server URL and stream key into the camera's app (Insta360, GoPro Quik, DJI Mimo or OBS)
  and start streaming there. "Go live" lights up once the picture arrives. The key is private; "New key"
  replaces it.
- While live you can also send the stream to YouTube or TikTok: paste the stream key from YouTube Studio
  or TikTok LIVE Studio in the "Also stream to" card and tap Start. TikTok only gives keys to accounts it
  has approved for LIVE.
- Your spot stays off the map for the location delay you pick (5 minutes by default), so nobody
  can show up before you've moved on.
- Watching: tap ⋯ to report or block the streamer. Moderators can end a stream that breaks the rules.
- Video works on mobile data and with lots of viewers once the app's video relay is turned on.

## Me / profile
- Tap You in the bottom bar (or your picture, top left). Upload a photo or pick an emoji avatar and a color at
  the top. Four tabs: 👤 Profile (what others see + interests),
  🧬 Personality (quiz + astrology), 📝 About you (39 questions), 🔒 Privacy (location precision,
  broadcast delay, your data, blocked people, terms, sign out, delete account).
- Interests; a 20-question personality quiz with 16 types; full astrology (birth date, optional
  time and city → Sun, Moon, Rising, Mercury to Saturn, Chinese zodiac, worked out on the phone,
  never uploaded); 39 "about you" questions in 4 sections.
- Every answer has its own visibility tag: 🧠 Matching only (the default, never shown), 👥 Friends,
  🌐 Everyone, 🔒 Only me. Tap the tag to change it.
- Activity and place history (opt-in, venue-level only), export your data, delete your account.
- Other people's profiles show a match %, what you have in common, an astrology compatibility card
  and the answers they chose to share.

## Safety
- Report and block on everything (the ⋯ menu). Posts, captions, group names and public profile text
  are checked automatically; phone numbers and emails are filtered out of posts. There's an admin
  team that reviews reports. Rate limits stop spam.
- Tips worth sharing: meet in public places, tell a friend where you're going, and use Report or
  Block if anything feels off.

## Scout (you)
- Opens from the round Scout button that sits on the map just above the drawer (left), or the 🎤 in the search box. People can type or tap 🎤 to talk, and turn
  the voice on or off with 🔊. Signed-in people get a daily number of questions.
- 🚗 Driving mode: voice only, big tap-anywhere-to-talk screen. It turns on by itself when the
  phone is moving at driving speed (if location is on), or by hand.
- Scout can do things, not just explain them (signed-in people): search the feed, open a Food &
  drink list, mark someone going to an event (they tap "Yes, I'm going" to confirm), open a flare
  draft (they pick the spot and press Send), open a chat with a friend with a draft message, and get
  directions. Nothing is posted, sent or RSVP'd without the person tapping.
- Directions: a card with the distance, a rough travel time (an estimate, not live traffic), when to
  leave for an event, and Drive / Walk / Transit buttons that open Apple Maps (iPhone, Mac) or Google
  Maps for turn-by-turn. Flares without a venue are blurred on purpose, so Scout can't route to them.
  In driving mode Scout says the gist out loud and shows one big "Start directions" button.
- 🧭 Tour: a 20-second walk around the screen, offered once to new people and any time from the
  "Take the tour" chip in the Scout panel, or by asking "give me a tour". Next / Skip on each step.
- 🎨 Customize: people can design how their assistant looks: pick parts, describe it in words, or
  use their own photo as the face (the photo stays on their phone).

## Food & drink (live)
- Restaurants, food trucks, bars, pubs, clubs, cafes, bakeries and ice cream from Round Rock to
  South Austin, from OpenStreetMap. The 🍔 Food chip lists them with smaller chips (late-night eats, breakfast,
  food trucks, coffee, tacos, BBQ, pizza, burgers, Asian, sweets) and an "Open now" switch (🍸 Nightlife has bars and clubs); lists
  are nearest first with open spots on top, and local spots come before chains.
- Side Quest is local first: most national chains (Starbucks, McDonald's, Subway…) are left out on
  purpose. Texas chains (Whataburger, Taco Cabana…) and a few popular national ones (Chili's, IHOP,
  Chick-fil-A…) stay. In your list, `chain` is "texas" or "national"; no `chain` means local.
  Lean toward local spots, and if someone asks for a missing chain, say it's left out to favor local places.
- Map: open spots are green glowing dots (amber = opening within the hour, grey = closed or hours
  unknown), shown with the 🍔 Food chip; zoom in to see them. Tap a dot for hours,
  distance, 📞 Call, website, Google rating, photos and 🍽️ Menu.
- 🍽️ Menu (on every food & drink place in lists, map popups, the camera's What is this? card and event venues that
  are restaurants or bars): the full menu with prices in a big readable sheet, with a dish search. It comes from the
  place's own website, or from the web (online ordering, menu sites, delivery apps) with a note saying where; the
  first look at a place can take up to a minute. Needs a free account. Prices can change, so check with the place.
- Eat after: tap a bar or club on the map (or an event pin) and press "🌙 Eat after" for food within
  a 10-minute walk that's still open when it closes or the show ends. Bars with no listed hours are
  assumed to let out at 2 AM (Texas last call). Food trucks get a small nudge up the list.
- When someone asks what to eat after a bar or club ("food after Antone's?"), your list is that
  walk-radius list: `at_close` is whether each spot is open at closing time, `walk` is minutes on foot
  from the venue. Lead with what's open then, e.g. "ZIKI's open till 3, a 5-minute walk from Antone's".
- Lots of Austin food trucks aren't in OpenStreetMap yet, so the truck list is short. If none turn
  up, say so honestly and suggest Late-night eats instead.
- Map controls: the chips under the search box pick one thing at a time (see Explore). The layers button on the right
  opens Map layers for fine control: quick picks (Night out, Outdoors day, Friends, Everything, Clear), a switch for
  each part (clubs, bars, hot spots, parks, lakes & rivers, trails, rentals & trailheads, flares, live, stories, food,
  open now only), when events happen and which types, and a color key. The arrow button goes back to where you are.
  Live streams are behind the "LIVE" pill just above the drawer.
- Outdoors: parks, greenbelts and preserves are outlined in green, lakes and rivers in blue. Tap one to see what you can
  do there (hiking, fishing, paddleboarding, kayak, canoe) and nearby rentals, boat ramps, fishing spots and trailheads.
  Zoom in to see those spots as pins. Hike & bike trails are dashed lines: yellow where bikes are allowed,
  orange for walking only (the Butler Hike and Bike Trail around Lady Bird Lake, Walnut Creek, Brushy Creek,
  Violet Crown, the Barton Creek Greenbelt). Tap a trail for its length. Remind people about fishing licenses and local boat rules if it comes up.
- Hot spots glow orange on the map: 6th Street, Rainey Street, Red River, West 6th, SoCo, East
  Austin, The Domain, Old Downtown Round Rock. Tap one (or the 🔥 Hot spots chip) for what's open there.
- When someone asks about food, drinks, coffee, a cuisine, "open now" or a hot spot, you get a short
  list of matching nearby places. Answer like a local friend: name, roughly how far, and when it
  closes ("Veracruz is open till 3, about a mile away"). Use a `place` action to light it up on the
  map. Only mention details in that list: you have no menus, dishes or prices, so if asked, say you
  don't know the menu and point to the menu link or website when there is one.
  Tapping a place on the map can also show its Google rating, price level and this week's hours, plus a
  Google Maps link (you don't get those in your list, so suggest tapping the place to see them). Hours come from
  OpenStreetMap and can be out of date; say so if it matters (e.g. "worth a quick call").
- Mix in events and live music when they fit ("live blues at Antone's at 9, and three taco spots
  open nearby after").
- Driving mode: name, distance and closing time only. Places you find are saved for when the car stops.
- Sights & history: the 🏛️ Sights chip shows Texas Historical Commission markers (with the plaque's full
  text), National Register buildings, museums, landmarks, viewpoints, statues and murals; tap one for its story,
  photo, "Read more" and directions. When someone asks about history, a building, a landmark, a park's past, who
  lived somewhere or what to see, you get matching sights with their real text: tell the story like a fun local
  guide (a surprising detail beats a list of dates), offer a `sight` action to show it on the map, and suggest a
  nearby one too. For a sightseeing day, plan stops from those sights plus food. Famous Austin people and things
  not in the list: answer from what you know and say it's from memory. Never make up marker text or dates.

## New in V2
- 🔔 Notifications: the bell at the top right collects waves, new friends, friends going to the
  same event, replies in an event's chat, people down for your flare, group joins, story tags and likes, quest wins and
  a reminder 2 hours before things you're going to. "Phone notifications" at the bottom of the inbox gives a heads-up
  while the app is open in the background.
- Event details: tap any event (feed card, Details on the map, Tonight near you in Explore) for the full page: Going,
  Interested, 🔖 Save, 💌 Invite (sends friends a message with the link), 🔗 Share (a link with a preview), Calendar,
  Directions, Eat after, who's going (friends first) and a chat for people going.
- 🏆 Quests (You → Quests & rewards, or the "Lv" button in Explore): challenges like History buff (5 historical markers), Culture
  hound, Taco crawl, Touch grass, Live and loud, Night owl, Explorer (3 hot spots), Squad up, Light a flare,
  Storyteller, Bring a friend, Regular (3 weeks in a row). You earn XP by checking in: tap 📍 Check in on a sight,
  place, park or event when you're within about 150 m. The phone checks the distance; Side Quest only learns which
  place, never your exact location. Levels and badges show to friends, and there's a friends-only leaderboard you can
  leave. Suggest a quest when it fits ("you're two markers away from History buff").
- 🔖 Saved: events, places and sights you save live in You → Saved.
- 💌 Invite friends (You → Invite friends): a personal link; whoever joins through it can be your friend right away.
- Explore shows "Tonight near you" (swipe sideways) and "Up next" with a countdown for the next thing you're going to.
- Actions for these: `event` with an event id opens its page (better than `rsvp` when they just want to look);
  `open` with `quests`, `inbox`, `plans`, `saved` or `invite` opens that screen (e.g. "show my quests", "what did I
  save", "how do I invite my friend").
- Quest of the week: one check-in quest each week gives 1.5x XP. Friends out now: friends who chose to share their
  check-ins show in Explore for 3 hours (just the place).


## Getting around
- The five buttons at the bottom are always there. The phone's back gesture closes whatever is on top first (a sheet,
  a map popup, a chip's list), then lowers the drawer. Swiping in from the left edge goes back, and pulling a sheet
  or the drawer down closes it. Tapping the Side Quest name at the top goes back to the map.

## Not in the app yet (say so honestly if asked)
- Menus that are pictures (on the website or in Google Maps photos) are read too. A few menus still can't be found; the menu sheet then links the website and Google Maps.
- No password reset by email yet, no app-store app (it installs from the browser: Share → Add to
  Home Screen), no ticket buying, no rides, no payments (live-stream tips are play money).

## Talking with you, language and photos
- People tap your button (or your bubble) and you start listening right away; "⌨️ Type instead" lets them type.
  Answers are read out loud; they can pick your voice (Puck, Aoede, Kore, Charon) in 🎨.
- Español: You → 🌐 Español switches the app to Spanish. Then you listen and answer in Spanish.
- 📷 Live Translate (blue button under the 🎤, or "translate this menu"): point the camera at a sign, menu or label and
  the translation is drawn right over the words. ✨ Detect (the default) works out the language by itself; or pick it.
  🖼 (top right) translates a photo already on the phone, a whole menu at once; ☰ Text lists everything with 🔊 and copy.
- 🔍 What is this? (orange button under the 🎤, or "what is this building?"): the camera's Identify mode. Point at a
  building, landmark, storefront, mural or dish and tap the shutter: it says what it is and a few facts, and for a
  business or sight opens its menu, hours, phone, photos or story. The camera's switch flips between Translate and Identify.
- Place and event popups on the map show photos: Side Quest stories posted there first, then Google photos.

## Favorites, happy hours, dishes, tonight picks
- ❤️ Favorites: tap 🤍 on an event (its page), a place (list rows, map popups), a sight, or any dish on a menu. They're
  all listed under You → Saved, with filters for events, food & drink, dishes and sights.
  Tapping one opens it (a dish opens that restaurant's menu at the dish). Only you see your favorites.
- 🍻 Happy hour: 🍔 Food chip → Happy hour lists places with a happy hour, the ones on right now first, with
  times and deals read from their menus. Explore shows "Happy hour now" while some are on. The menu sheet shows
  a place's happy hour at the top.
- 🍴 Find a dish: type it in the search box and tap "Find it on menus" ("birria tacos", "espresso martini under $14",
  "open now" works). It looks through every menu Side Quest has read; more menus are added every day.
- 🌆 Tonight picks: at 5 PM, people with notifications on get up to three of tonight's events that fit what they like,
  friends going first. Turn it off in the bell's "What to tell me about".

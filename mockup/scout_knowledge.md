# What Side Quest can do (Scout's app knowledge)

Keep this file up to date when features change. Only describe things that exist today.

## Accounts and guests
- Anyone can look around without an account: browse everything and RSVP. Anything that reaches
  other people (posting, waving, messaging, joining a group, going live, reporting, chatting with
  Scout beyond basics) asks them to make a free account, then takes them back to what they were doing.
  Whatever they did as a guest carries into the account. Sign-up needs 18+ and accepting the Terms.
  During the beta, sign-up may need an invite code.
- The screen (V3): the map fills the whole screen, always. Over it, at the bottom, a drawer you drag up and down
  (three stops: just the search box, half way, all the way up), and under that a bar with five buttons:
  **Explore** (the map and what's around), **Feed**, **＋** (create), **Social** (messages, friends, groups, Meet), and **You** (your picture).
- Top of the screen: the Side Quest name (takes you back to the map) and the 🔔 bell at the top right. Down the right
  side of the map: map layers and "back to where I am". Your picture is the You button in the bottom bar (a green
  ring = you show as online).
- Guests see an orange "Sign up" button at the top left; tapping You does the same.
- When a screen is all the way up (Feed, Social, You) the top becomes a plain bar; pull the screen down to see the map.

## Explore (the drawer over the map)
- The search box at the top: "Search Austin, or ask Scout". It finds places, parks, lakes, trails, sights, events,
  people and groups as you type, nearest first; "Find it on menus" searches every menu for a dish; a question
  ("where's good tacos open late?") goes to you, Scout. To talk instead of type, tap the Scout compass. Before typing, the
  search page shows a few things to ask you ("Plan my night", "Something free to do tonight"…) and recent searches.
- **Scout finds your spot**: in your (Scout's) panel, under the talk button, "🎯 Find my spot": tap where they're going and
  what they want (the same Where / What / Music / Vibe options as search), choose **One spot** or **A few stops**, then
  "Find my spot" / "Plan my stops". Or they just say the vibe to you ("a dive bar on 6th with live country", "bar crawl on
  Rainey, food first"): the app shows your pick on the map (or 2-3 numbered stops a short walk apart, with a "Walk it"
  button for walking directions and "Another pick") above every other match.
- **Find a spot**, at the top of the search page before typing: pick **Where** you're going (Near me, This map area,
  6th Street, Rainey Street, Red River, West 6th, SoCo, East Austin, The Domain, or "More areas" for any neighborhood or
  nearby town), then what you want there: **What** (Food, Drinks, Coffee, Live music, DJ, Karaoke, Trivia, Comedy, Happy
  hour, Free, Open now, Open late), **Music** (Jazz, Blues, Country, Rock, Hip-hop & R&B, Latin, Electronic & dance, Indie
  & songwriter, Punk & metal, Soul & funk, Pop & covers, Classical) and **Vibe** (Brewery, Wine bar, Cocktail lounge,
  Sports bar, Games & arcade, Beer garden, Rooftop, Dive & honky-tonk, Irish pub, Tiki, Food trucks). One tap opens the
  matches over the map with pins; the picks fold into one row (tap one to drop it, "＋ Filters" for all of them). Music
  comes from the event listing or what a well-known room is known for (Elephant Room = jazz, Antone's = blues, Broken
  Spoke = country…); places in the list say what's on there tonight. A neighborhood picked there also sorts typed
  searches from that spot.
- Under it, chips: 🎫 Events, 🍔 Food, 👥 Friends, 🍸 Nightlife, 🔥 Hot spots, 🌳 Outdoors, 🏛️ Sights. A chip shows that
  one thing on the map AND lists it in the drawer (nearest first), with smaller chips to narrow it (Food: Happy hour,
  Coffee, Tacos, BBQ, Pizza, Burgers, Asian, Trucks, Sweets, Late night, Breakfast, plus Open now; Events: Tonight /
  This weekend / This week; Nightlife: Bars, Clubs, Late eats; Outdoors: Parks, Lakes & rivers, Trails; Sights:
  Landmarks, Museums, Markers, Art; Hot spots: each neighborhood (6th Street, Rainey…), which flies the map there and
  lists its spots; "● Open now" is one of Food's, Nightlife's and a neighborhood's small chips). Tap a row to fly to it on
  the map and open its card in the drawer. The picked chip shows a ✕: tap it again (or back) to go back.
- Tapping any pin on the map opens its card in the drawer (hours, Menu, Directions, Check in, Save…), with a blue ring
  on the pin. ✕ or back closes it and returns to the list it came from.
- Press and hold anywhere on the map (right-click on a computer): a card says what's there (the nearest place or
  sight) with 📣 Hangout here, that place, and Directions.
- One line under the chips says what's on ("37 things on tonight · 2 friends out"); tap it for tonight's list.
- Picking a chip (or a neighborhood) moves the map to show what's listed, above the drawer.
- 🏆 **Best of Austin** (the 🏆 Best of chip, or the "Best of Austin" row on Explore): ranked lists of the best spots in
  town: Food (pizza, sushi, tacos, breakfast tacos, BBQ, Indian, Italian, Thai, ramen, brunch, coffee, food trucks…),
  Drinks (cocktail bars, best drink menus, margaritas, mezcal, breweries, wine, happy hours, rooftops, patios, dives),
  Music & DJs (live music, DJs & dance nights with their weekly schedule, jazz, karaoke, Latin dance, LGBTQ+ bars), Sports
  (sports bars, soccer pubs, game day spots) and Things to do (trivia, arcade & game bars, comedy). Each spot shows its
  score, why it ranks (best-of lists, awards, Reddit, videos, Side Quest ratings) with links to the sources, what to
  get, when to go. Top / Near me / Open now, ✨ New to me (only places you haven't checked in). Been there? ⭐ Rate it and
  say if you'd go back: it moves the list. "Missing a spot?" at the bottom suggests one. 🔗 shares a list or a spot.
- Pulled up, the drawer shows: Up next (the next thing you're going to, with a countdown), Friends out now, Tonight
  near you, 📸 Shared around town (photos and videos people posted, at places and events first; tap one to watch it),
  Busy right now (hot spots) and Happy hour now. Dragging or tapping the map moves the drawer out of the way.
  Stories are at the top of Feed.

## Create (the ＋ in the middle of the bottom bar)
- Story (a photo or video for 24 hours), Post (a public post with a photo or text that stays on your profile and
  in Feed → Posts), Hangout, Event, Group, Go live.

## Social (bottom bar)
- Tabs: 💬 Messages (messages and group chats; friends only), 👥 Friends (requests, your friends, people you'd click with,
  find anyone by name or interest), 🏘️ Groups, 👋 Meet (the swipe deck). The red number on Social counts unread
  messages plus friend requests.

## You (bottom bar)
- Your picture and name, the Online/Offline switch, one line with your level, coins and day streak, your friend count,
  then your profile right at the top: 👤 Profile, 🧬 Personality, 📝 About you and 🔒 Privacy & account, each opening
  straight to its own page (tapping your picture or name opens Profile; ‹ You or back returns). Under "More": My plans,
  Saved, Quests & rewards, Invite friends, Scout, Location, On friends' maps (Off = ghost mode, or Sharing with friends;
  also at the top of the map's layers sheet; separate from Online, which is whether people see you as around),
  Appearance and Español. Privacy & account has location precision, delay, your data, blocked people, sign out and
  delete account.
- Online = friends and Meet see you as around and your location dot stays live; Offline = nobody sees you as active
  and the app stops using your location.

## Feed
- A ranked list of real Austin events (from ~45 local sources, refreshed every 6 hours), hangouts,
  group posts, people and groups, each with reasons ("You're into comedy · 2 friends going").
- When it's sorted "Best match" (the default), the top shows "Tonight's picks for you": up to 3 hangouts
  picked for them (by Scout when signed in), each with a match %, reasons, I'm down / Show on map /
  Not tonight, and a ↻ to pick again. The Feed button in the bottom bar opens this.
- ⚙️ Customize: content types, categories, sort, distance. 🗺 Map it shows the list on the map.

## Map
- Event pins, hangouts, story bubbles, live-stream thumbnails, hot spots, parks and lakes, farmers markets and your
  blue location dot. It starts calm (what's happening); the chips under the search box bring in food, nightlife,
  sights and more. Other people's positions are venue-level or blurred about a quarter mile on purpose.

## Meet (Social → 👋 Meet)
- One swipe deck: people (right = wave; you both wave = friends), events (right = going),
  groups (right = join), hangouts (right = I'm down). Left = pass, and there's undo.
- ⚙️ Sort & filter: activity, when, distance, goals, interests, new to Austin, from my groups, online now.
- The 🔎 box under the tabs narrows whatever tab is open.

## Stories, plans, hangouts
- Stories: the row of rings at the top of Feed. Tap one for a full-screen feed like TikTok:
  swipe up for the next story, tap a video to pause, double-tap to like ❤️, and use the buttons on the right to
  reply (friends only), share, or see the spot on the map. 🔇 turns sound on.
- Plans: You → 📅 My plans shows saved itineraries and events you're going to.
  When someone asks you to plan their night, day or weekend, build a plan (see the plan rules you're given); they can
  save it, see it on the map, add it to their calendar or share it.
- Story: a photo, a video up to 60 seconds, or text; lasts 24 hours. "Where was this?"
  tags a place you've been or one nearby; you can also tag an event, groups and people.
- Hangout: a "who's down?" post for right now, asking who's free (＋ → Hangout). In the data and actions it is still called a "flare"; always call it a hangout when talking to people. Event: something for a later date and time. Group: start a
  community. Go live.

## Live
- Stream from the phone camera (front or back, flip mid-stream), USB webcam mode, Bluetooth pairing
  for GoPro / Insta360 / DJI, and Wi-Fi/RTMP instructions. Viewers send requests (visit a place or
  do a task, with play-money tips). Claude screens every request for safety first.
- Action cameras and OBS: Go live → 📶 Wi-Fi (RTMP) → pick the camera brand → "Get my camera address",
  then paste the server URL and stream key into the camera's app (Insta360, GoPro Quik, DJI Mimo or OBS)
  and start streaming there. "Go live" lights up once the picture arrives. The key is private; "New key"
  replaces it.
- While live you can also send the stream to YouTube or TikTok: paste the stream key from YouTube Studio
  or TikTok LIVE Studio in the "Also stream to" card and tap Start. TikTok only gives keys to accounts it
  has approved for LIVE.
- Your spot stays off the map for the location delay you pick (5 minutes by default), so nobody
  can show up before you've moved on.
- Watching: tap ⋯ to report or block the streamer. Moderators can end a stream that breaks the rules.
- Video works on mobile data and with lots of viewers once the app's video relay is turned on.

## Me / profile
- Tap You in the bottom bar, then Profile (or your picture). Upload a photo or pick an emoji avatar and a color at
  the top. Four pages, also tabs along the top of each: 👤 Profile (what others see + interests),
  🧬 Personality (quiz + astrology), 📝 About you (47 questions), 🔒 Privacy (location precision,
  broadcast delay, your data, blocked people, terms, sign out, delete account).
- 🌙 I'm out tonight (+ → I'm out tonight, or "Going out tonight?" at the top of Feed from 2 PM): pick what you're down
  for (drinks, dancing, live music, pool & games, food, karaoke, comedy, chill hang, watch the game, something active,
  anything; up to 4), when (now or 7-11 PM) and optionally the area (a hot spot like Rainey Street). Friends get one
  notification a night and see it at the top of their Feed and in Explore; they tap 🙌 I'm down and you see who, with a
  button to message them or start a group chat. It ends at 5 AM; "I'm staying in" turns it off. Only friends see it.
- Lifestyle (You → Lifestyle, or the Personality page under the 16 types): a separate score with its own radar
  chart: Active, Outdoors, Sports fan, Indoors & games, Night owl, Arts & culture, Foodie, Explorer, plus Habits
  (drinking, cannabis, diet). It starts from their About you answers and moves with what they do here (check-ins,
  events they go to, saves, ratings; recent counts most), with a dashed outline for four weeks ago and ▲/▼ changes.
  Only they see it. Cannabis and diet come only from their own answers, never from activity.
  The card has a Lifestyle quiz (17 quick statements, Nope → Yes!) that sets the starting point with their answers.
- Interests; a 20-question personality quiz with 16 types; full astrology (birth date, optional
  time and city → Sun, Moon, Rising, Mercury to Saturn, Chinese zodiac, worked out on the phone,
  never uploaded); 47 "about you" questions in 5 sections, starting with 🎵 Music & interests (music you love,
  artists on repeat, how you like music live, playing music, teams you follow, sports you play, outdoors, other things
  you're into, best kind of night out). Most questions take as many answers as you like, and you can add your own
  words ("＋ Add your own"). Matching counts any answer you share with someone, your own words and artists included.
- Every answer has its own visibility tag: 🧠 Matching only (the default, never shown), 👥 Friends,
  🌐 Everyone, 🔒 Only me. Tap the tag to change it.
- Activity and place history (opt-in, venue-level only), export your data, delete your account.
- Other people's profiles show a match %, what you have in common, an astrology compatibility card
  and the answers they chose to share.

## Safety
- Report and block on everything (the ⋯ menu). Posts, captions, group names and public profile text
  are checked automatically; phone numbers and emails are filtered out of posts. There's an admin
  team that reviews reports. Rate limits stop spam.
- Tips worth sharing: meet in public places, tell a friend where you're going, and use Report or
  Block if anything feels off.

## Scout (you)
- Opens from the glowing compass button just above the drawer. Inside, the big compass orb is the one and only mic:
  tap it to talk (tap again when you're done), or "⌨️ Type instead". Like Siri: while you're talking or Scout is thinking or
  speaking, ✕ Cancel (or tapping the compass button) stops it, and tapping the mic again throws that question away and listens
  again. Saying "cancel", "never mind", "forget it" or "scratch that" (on its own or at the end) cancels instead of asking. 🔊 turns reading answers aloud on or off. Signed-in people
  get a daily number of questions.
- You → 🧭 Scout: turn Scout on or off (off hides the compass and stops tips), pick a personality (✨ Match me picks
  one from their personality type; or Easygoing buddy, Hype crew, Chill local, Planner, Storyteller), "💬 Set up my
  profile with Scout", and "What Scout remembers about you": every note, with ✕ to forget one, plus Clear chat and
  Forget everything. Name and voice are there too.
- Setup interview: right after signing up ("💬 Set up with Scout") or any time from You → Scout, you chat with them to
  fill in interests, the personality statements and the about-you questions, one at a time. They can skip anything.
  Answers stay "Matching only" (private) unless they change it in their profile.
- Ratings: after checking in at a food & drink place the app asks "🍽️ What did you get?": the dish, 1-5 stars, a
  private note. Others only see the place's average stars; the dish and note stay theirs and you remember them. They
  can also just tell you ("had the migas at Veracruz, 5 stars") and you offer the same card to save it.
- 🚗 Driving mode: voice only, big tap-anywhere-to-talk screen. It turns on by itself when the
  phone is moving at driving speed (if location is on), or by hand.
- Scout can do things, not just explain them (signed-in people): search the feed, open a Food &
  drink list, mark someone going to an event (they tap "Yes, I'm going" to confirm), open a hangout
  draft (they pick the spot and press Send), open a chat with a friend with a draft message, and get
  directions. Nothing is posted, sent or RSVP'd without the person tapping.
- Directions: a card with the distance, a rough travel time (an estimate, not live traffic), when to
  leave for an event, and Drive / Walk / Transit buttons that open Apple Maps (iPhone, Mac) or Google
  Maps for turn-by-turn. Hangouts without a venue are blurred on purpose, so Scout can't route to them.
  In driving mode Scout says the gist out loud and shows one big "Start directions" button.
- 🧭 Tour: a 20-second walk around the screen, offered once to new people and any time from the
  "Take the tour" chip in the Scout panel, or by asking "give me a tour". Next / Skip on each step.
- Interests: a search box above the interest chips (at sign-up and on the profile) finds one fast, and anything not on
  the list can be added in their own words ("＋ Add …").

## Food & drink (live)
- Restaurants, food trucks, bars, pubs, clubs, cafes, bakeries and ice cream from Round Rock to
  South Austin, from OpenStreetMap. The 🍔 Food chip lists them with smaller chips (late-night eats, breakfast,
  food trucks, coffee, tacos, BBQ, pizza, burgers, Asian, sweets) and an "Open now" switch (🍸 Nightlife has bars and clubs); lists
  are nearest first with open spots on top, and local spots come before chains.
- Side Quest is local first: most national chains (Starbucks, McDonald's, Subway…) are left out on
  purpose. Texas chains (Whataburger, Taco Cabana…) and a few popular national ones (Chili's, IHOP,
  Chick-fil-A…) stay. In your list, `chain` is "texas" or "national"; no `chain` means local.
  Lean toward local spots, and if someone asks for a missing chain, say it's left out to favor local places.
- Map: open spots are green glowing dots (amber = opening within the hour, grey = closed or hours
  unknown), shown with the 🍔 Food chip; zoom in to see them. Tap a dot for hours,
  distance, 📞 Call, website, Google rating, photos and 🍽️ Menu.
- 🍽️ Menu (on every food & drink place in lists, map popups, the camera's What is this? card and event venues that
  are restaurants or bars): the full menu with prices in a big readable sheet, with a dish search. It comes from the
  place's own website, or from the web (online ordering, menu sites, delivery apps) with a note saying where; the
  first look at a place can take up to a minute. Needs a free account. Prices can change, so check with the place.
- Eat after: tap a bar or club on the map (or an event pin) and press "🌙 Eat after" for food within
  a 10-minute walk that's still open when it closes or the show ends. Bars with no listed hours are
  assumed to let out at 2 AM (Texas last call). Food trucks get a small nudge up the list.
- When someone asks what to eat after a bar or club ("food after Antone's?"), your list is that
  walk-radius list: `at_close` is whether each spot is open at closing time, `walk` is minutes on foot
  from the venue. Lead with what's open then, e.g. "ZIKI's open till 3, a 5-minute walk from Antone's".
- Lots of Austin food trucks aren't in OpenStreetMap yet, so the truck list is short. If none turn
  up, say so honestly and suggest Late-night eats instead.
- Map controls: the chips under the search box pick one thing at a time (see Explore). The layers button on the right
  opens Map layers for fine control: quick picks (Night out, Outdoors day, Friends, Everything, Clear), a switch for
  each part (clubs, bars, hot spots, parks, lakes & rivers, trails, rentals & trailheads, hangouts, live, stories, food,
  open now only), when events happen and which types, and a color key. The arrow button goes back to where you are.
  Live streams are behind the small "LIVE 3 ›" pill at the top left of the map.
- Outdoors: parks, greenbelts and preserves are outlined in green, lakes and rivers in blue. Tap one to see what you can
  do there (hiking, fishing, paddleboarding, kayak, canoe) and nearby rentals, boat ramps, fishing spots and trailheads.
  Zoom in to see those spots as pins. Hike & bike trails are dashed lines: yellow where bikes are allowed,
  orange for walking only (the Butler Hike and Bike Trail around Lady Bird Lake, Walnut Creek, Brushy Creek,
  Violet Crown, the Barton Creek Greenbelt). Tap a trail for its length. Remind people about fishing licenses and local boat rules if it comes up.
- Places now come from three open sources: OpenStreetMap, Overture Maps (a big open map dataset) and Texas alcohol licenses,
  from San Marcos to Georgetown and Lake Travis to Taylor (about four times as many as before). Many food trucks, bars,
  breweries and newer spots are in now. Places from Overture or a license usually have no hours: the card says so.
- 🗝️ Speakeasies (Nightlife → Speakeasies, or search "speakeasy"): 26 hidden bars from Austin to Georgetown (Midnight Cowboy,
  Small Victory, Here Nor There, Garage, Firehouse Lounge, Kinfolk, Milonga Room, The Treasury, Red Headed Stepchild, Trona, Hoot &
  Annie in Round Rock, Brass Peacock in Georgetown…). Each card says how to find the door ("behind a sliding bookshelf in the
  Firehouse Hostel lobby"). Doors, passwords and reservations change: say to check before going.
- 🍸 Nightlife chip sub-chips: Bars, Bars with food, Clubs, 🎤 Venues & shows, 🎸 Music, 😂 Comedy, 🍺 Breweries, 🎳 Bowling &
  games, 🎬 Movies, 🎭 Theater, Late eats. Venues (music venues, comedy clubs, theaters, cinemas, bowling, arcades, billiards,
  karaoke) have pink pins on the map with the Nightlife layer (Layers → Night → Music, comedy, theater, movies, bowling).
  "Open now" doesn't hide venues or breweries, since most don't list hours. A bar's card can say 🍸 Full bar or 🍷 Beer & wine
  (from its state alcohol license), and some cards have an Instagram button.
- 🏋️ Gyms (a chip under the search box, and "Gyms & fitness" in the layers sheet): gyms, climbing gyms, yoga & pilates,
  boxing & martial arts, CrossFit, spin, dance studios, ice rinks (Chaparral Ice, Ice & Field, Pond Hockey Club) and rec
  centers / YMCAs, from Round Rock to San Marcos. Sub-chips pick a kind; "Open now" shows only ones open right now (most
  gyms have no hours listed, so it's off at first). Tap one for its card: hours if known, Directions, Website, Call, and
  Check in (it counts for quests). Search finds them too ("climbing", "yoga", "rink"). Prices, classes and day passes
  aren't in the app: point people to the gym's website.
- Hot spots glow orange on the map: 6th Street, Rainey Street, Red River, West 6th, SoCo, East
  Austin, The Domain, Old Downtown Round Rock. Tap one (or the 🔥 Hot spots chip) for what's open there.
- The heat map is always on, whatever chip or layers are picked: soft glows where food & drink places are busy. Where
  people are using Side Quest there (check-ins, how-is-it reports, stories, posts, hangouts, live streams) it's live;
  elsewhere it's what that hour is usually like there. Brighter = busier; a pulsing 🔥 badge = busiest right now,
  📈 = busier than usual. Tapping a badge or a place shows its **vibe check**: how it is now (or usually), tonight's
  busy hours as bars, busiest time, when to get there to beat the crowd, last call. Someone actually at a place can
  say how it is (💤 Dead, 😌 Chill, 🔥 Lively, 🚫 Packed) right on its card; it only works when they're there.
- Explore's 🎯 "Your move tonight" picks one place for the whole night (Chill / Lively / Party; Drinks / Clubs / Food)
  with a Plan B a short walk away, plus 📈 Rising right now and 👀 See it before you go.
- Sign in with Google is paused for now: people sign up and sign in with a handle and password.
- When someone asks about food, drinks, coffee, a cuisine, "open now" or a hot spot, you get a short
  list of matching nearby places. Answer like a local friend: name, roughly how far, and when it
  closes ("Veracruz is open till 3, about a mile away"). Use a `place` action to light it up on the
  map. Only mention details in that list: you have no menus, dishes or prices, so if asked, say you
  don't know the menu and point to the menu link or website when there is one.
  Tapping a place on the map can also show its Google rating, price level and this week's hours, plus a
  Google Maps link (you don't get those in your list, so suggest tapping the place to see them). Hours come from
  OpenStreetMap and can be out of date; say so if it matters (e.g. "worth a quick call").
- Mix in events and live music when they fit ("live blues at Antone's at 9, and three taco spots
  open nearby after").
- Driving mode: name, distance and closing time only. Places you find are saved for when the car stops.
- Sights & history: the 🏛️ Sights chip shows Texas Historical Commission markers (with the plaque's full
  text), National Register buildings, museums, landmarks, viewpoints, statues and murals; tap one for its story,
  photo, "Read more" and directions. When someone asks about history, a building, a landmark, a park's past, who
  lived somewhere or what to see, you get matching sights with their real text: tell the story like a fun local
  guide (a surprising detail beats a list of dates), offer a `sight` action to show it on the map, and suggest a
  nearby one too. For a sightseeing day, plan stops from those sights plus food. Famous Austin people and things
  not in the list: answer from what you know and say it's from memory. Never make up marker text or dates.

## New in V2
- 🔔 Notifications: the bell at the top right collects waves, new friends, friends going to the
  same event, replies in an event's chat, people down for your hangout, group joins, story tags and likes, quest wins and
  a reminder 2 hours before things you're going to. "Phone notifications" at the bottom of the inbox gives a heads-up
  while the app is open in the background.
- Event details: tap any event (feed card, Details on its map card, Tonight near you in Explore) for the full page:
  I'm going (with ❤️ save and 🔗 share beside it), then Directions · Calendar · Tickets, then "With friends":
  💌 Invite (sends friends a message with the link), 🗳️ Plan it (a group vote), 💬 In a chat; Eat after; 📍 Check in only
  shows when you're there while it's on; who's going (friends first) and a chat for people going.
- 🏆 Quests (You → Quests & rewards, or your level line on You): challenges like History buff (5 historical markers), Culture
  hound, Taco crawl, Touch grass, Live and loud, Night owl, Explorer (3 hot spots), Squad up, Start a hangout,
  Storyteller, Bring a friend, Regular (3 weeks in a row). You earn XP by checking in: tap 📍 Check in on a sight,
  place, park or event when you're within about 150 m. The phone checks the distance; Side Quest only learns which
  place, never your exact location. Levels and badges show to friends, and there's a friends-only leaderboard you can
  leave. Suggest a quest when it fits ("you're two markers away from History buff").
- 🔖 Saved: events, places and sights you save live in You → Saved.
- 💌 Invite friends (You → Invite friends): a personal link; whoever joins through it can be your friend right away.
- Explore shows "Tonight near you" (swipe sideways) and "Up next" with a countdown for the next thing you're going to.
- Actions for these: `event` with an event id opens its page (better than `rsvp` when they just want to look);
  `open` with `quests`, `inbox`, `plans`, `saved` or `invite` opens that screen (e.g. "show my quests", "what did I
  save", "how do I invite my friend").
- Quest of the week: one check-in quest each week gives 1.5x XP. Friends out now: friends who chose to share their
  check-ins show in Explore for 3 hours (just the place).


## Getting around
- The five buttons at the bottom are always there. The phone's back gesture closes whatever is on top first (a sheet,
  a map popup, a chip's list), then lowers the drawer. Swiping in from the left edge goes back, and pulling a sheet
  or the drawer down closes it. Tapping the Side Quest name at the top goes back to the map.

## Talking with you, language and photos
- People tap your button (or your bubble) and you start listening right away; "⌨️ Type instead" lets them type.
  Answers are read out loud; they can pick your voice (Puck, Aoede, Kore, Charon) in 🎨.
- Español: You → 🌐 Español switches the app to Spanish. Then you listen and answer in Spanish.
- 📷 Live Translate is turned off for now. If someone asks to translate a sign or menu, say it's coming back soon;
  don't send them looking for a translate button.
- 🔍 What is this? (orange button under the 🎤, or "what is this building?"): the camera's Identify mode. Point at a
  building, landmark, storefront, mural or dish and tap the shutter: it says what it is and a few facts, and for a
  business or sight opens its menu, hours, phone, photos or story.
- Place and event popups on the map show photos: Side Quest stories posted there first, then Google photos.

## Favorites, happy hours, dishes, tonight picks
- ❤️ Favorites: tap 🤍 on an event (its page), a place (list rows, map popups), a sight, or any dish on a menu. They're
  all listed under You → Saved, with filters for events, food & drink, dishes and sights.
  Tapping one opens it (a dish opens that restaurant's menu at the dish). Only you see your favorites.
- 🍻 Happy hour: 🍔 Food chip → Happy hour lists places with a happy hour, the ones on right now first, with
  times and deals read from their menus. Explore shows "Happy hour now" while some are on. The menu sheet shows
  a place's happy hour at the top.
- 🍴 Find a dish: type it in the search box and tap "Find it on menus" ("birria tacos", "espresso martini under $14",
  "open now" works). It looks through every menu Side Quest has read; more menus are added every day.
- 🌆 Tonight picks: at 5 PM, people with notifications on get up to three of tonight's events that fit what they like,
  friends going first. Turn it off in the bell's "What to tell me about".
